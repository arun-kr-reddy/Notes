## Don't Track Every Rupee

- energy better spent on upskilling and keeping yourself healthy & happy

## Law of Diminishing Marginal Utility
- each additional unit consumed yields less extra utility than the previous unit
- *e.g.* ₹0 → ₹10L transforms life but ₹10Cr → ₹10.1Cr barely changes anything
